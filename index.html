ABOUT OUR PLATFORM

What is LIFELINK GLOBAL?

Technology and community working together to support healthcare awareness and life-saving services.

Building a Connected Healthcare Community

LIFELINK GLOBAL is an innovative healthcare coordination concept that brings donors, recipients, hospitals, blood banks and local communities together through a single digital platform.

The platform aims to simplify access to healthcare information, promote voluntary donation awareness and improve communication between communities and authorised healthcare institutions.

With AI-assisted information support, map services, multilingual features and a future-ready architecture, LIFELINK GLOBAL aims to make healthcare coordination more accessible.

SMART TECHNOLOGY

AI-Assisted Healthcare Information

LIFELINK GLOBAL plans to use artificial intelligence to improve access to general healthcare information, support multilingual communication and guide users through platform services.

AI is intended to assist users with information. It will not replace doctors or make medical, donation eligibility or organ allocation decisions.
